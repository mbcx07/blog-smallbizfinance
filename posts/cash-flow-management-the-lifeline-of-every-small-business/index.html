Financial Management Principles

Sound financial management is the foundation of any successful business. Understanding cash flow, maintaining accurate books, and planning for taxes are non-negotiable skills for every entrepreneur.

Action Steps

  1. Separate Personal and Business Finances: Open dedicated business accounts from day one.
  2. Build a Financial Forecast: Project your revenue, expenses, and cash flow for the next 12 months.
  3. Establish Credit: Build your business credit profile early, even if you don't need financing yet.
  4. Monitor Key Metrics: Track gross margin, burn rate, and customer acquisition cost.
  5. Plan for Taxes: Set aside money for taxes with every payment you receive.
